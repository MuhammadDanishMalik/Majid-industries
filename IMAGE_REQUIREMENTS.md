## 📸 Image Specifications

### Product Photos
- **Format:** PNG with transparent background
- **Size:** 1000×1000px minimum
- **Background:** White or transparent
- **Lighting:** Consistent, professional
- **Angle:** Straight-on or 3/4 view
- **Quality:** High resolution

### Category/Lifestyle Photos
- **Format:** JPG
- **Size:** 800×600px minimum
- **Style:** Lifestyle or studio photography
- **Quality:** High resolution
- **Composition:** Professional, well-lit

### Logo Files
- **Format:** PNG with transparent background
- **Size:** 180×60px (or higher resolution, will scale)
- **Background:** Transparent
- **Quality:** Vector-based or high-res

### Certification Badges
- **Format:** PNG
- **Size:** 48×48px minimum
- **Background:** Transparent or white
- **Quality:** Clear, readable

---

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

1. **Product Photography:**
   - Use consistent white or transparent background
   - Maintain same angle and lighting across all products
   - Shoot in high resolution (can scale down)
   - Remove any shadows or reflections

2. **Lifestyle/Category Images:**
   - Show products in real-world context
   - Use natural or professional lighting
   - Maintain brand colors and aesthetic
   - High quality, sharp focus

3. **Logo:**
   - Provide highest resolution available
   - Transparent background essential
   - White version needed for dark backgrounds

4. **File Naming:**
   - Use lowercase
   - Use hyphens, not spaces
   - Be descriptive
   - Match exactly as listed above

## 🔄 How to Add Images

1. Save images with exact file names listed above
2. Place in correct folders as specified
3. Restart development server if running
4. Refresh browser to see changes
